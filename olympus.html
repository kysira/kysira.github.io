What Olympus does.

⇩Import what you have

Zone files, provider exports, CSV or hostname lists, with a diff preview before commit. Authorized AXFR/IXFR with TSIG, plus passive discovery.

⚠Finds the risky records

Dangling CNAMEs, takeover fingerprints, delegation problems, and domain expiry, resolved with CNAME chains and NXDOMAIN vs SERVFAIL kept distinct.

✉Alerts you can act on

Deduplicated alerts with a lifecycle, a triage queue, and notification channels with a delivery log.

⊟Isolated per tenant

Row-level security enforced in the database. Every change is audited and every record keeps its history.

Guardrails
